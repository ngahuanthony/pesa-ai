I would not make the limit purely “videos per day.” Make it a monthly Scan Credits allowance, with a daily protection limit.

Recommended structure
Package	Video scans / month	Daily soft limit	Best for
Starter	10	2/day	Small vendors
Business	40	5/day	Regular shops
Pro	100	10/day	Larger inventory
Enterprise	300+	Custom	Large businesses

A scan could have a maximum duration, e.g. 2 minutes, so one customer can't upload a 45-minute video and consume enormous processing resources.

🔥 Even better: automatic upgrade based on usage

Don't force the vendor to manually choose a package.

For example:

Starter — 10 scans

8/10 scans used
2 scans remaining

When they attempt scan #11:

🚀 You've reached your Starter scan limit.

Upgrade to Business to get 40 inventory scans/month.

Upgrade

This creates a very natural upgrade path.

But I would make one important change

Don't automatically charge/upgrade someone without their approval.

Instead:

You've exceeded your current plan. Move to Business?

Then they approve the upgrade.

You can, however, automatically recommend the next package based on their usage.

💡 The really clever pricing model

Eventually, don't think of it as:

“You get 40 videos.”

Think:

“You get 40 AI Inventory Scans.”

Because later you could have different scan types:

📹 Video Scan = 1 credit
📦 Product Scan = 0.25 credit
📄 Bulk Image Scan = 0.5 credit
🔄 Stock Re-scan = 1 credit

That gives you much more flexibility.

Example

A vendor has 40 Scan Credits.

They use:

10 shop scans

20 product scans
10 image scans

The system calculates the usage accordingly.

I would also put a fair-use ceiling on each scan

For example:

Maximum video: 90 seconds or 2 minutes

Maximum resolution: automatically compress before processing.

Maximum file size: perhaps 50–100 MB after compression.

This is important because your costs are driven much more by video length, frames processed, AI calls and storage than simply the number of uploads.
